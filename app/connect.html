Connect / Reconnect Hermes Accounting

Hermes Accounting is a private internal tool used by Exterior Art to read its own QuickBooks Online accounting data (read-only) for billing reconciliation.

To connect:
  1. Sign in to the Intuit account that has access to the Exterior Art QuickBooks Online company.
  2. Authorize Hermes Accounting for Accounting (read) access when prompted.
  3. Once authorized, the tool runs locally and refreshes its own access automatically.

Access can be reviewed at any time in QuickBooks Online under Settings → Apps → Connected Apps.
