Hermes Accounting

A private accounting-integration tool by Exterior Art (Randall Beard).

Hermes Accounting is an internal, single-company application that connects to Exterior Art's own QuickBooks Online account to read accounts-receivable and billing information for the purpose of reconciling and reporting on the business's invoices. It is not a public or multi-tenant product and is not offered for sale or download.

What it does
  • Reads accounts-receivable (A/R) aging, invoices, customers, and payment data from QuickBooks Online.
  • Compares those figures against the business's internal records and flags discrepancies.
  • Read-only. It never creates, changes, or deletes anything in QuickBooks.
