Disconnect Hermes Accounting

You can revoke Hermes Accounting's access to QuickBooks Online at any time. Disconnecting immediately stops all access; the locally stored tokens are deleted and the tool can no longer read any data.

To disconnect:
  1. In QuickBooks Online, go to Settings (gear) → Apps → Connected Apps.
  2. Find Hermes Accounting and choose Disconnect.
  3. That's it — access is revoked. The local tool will no longer be able to connect.

After disconnecting, no further accounting data can be accessed unless the app is connected again from the Connect page.
