Contact & Case Intake

To retain Randy Beard as an expert witness or consultant, or to ask whether he can help with a pool, landscape or hardscape problem, contact him directly. There is no web form; email reaches him personally.

How to reach Randy

  • Phone: (949) 264-3466
  • Email: rb@varx1.com
  • Based in: Costa Mesa, Orange County, California
  • Serves: Orange County, Los Angeles, San Diego and the Riverside area, with site inspections, depositions and trial testimony available nationwide
  • LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/rlbeard · YouTube: youtube.com/@varx1com

What to include in your first message

  • The property city, and the kind of structure involved (pool, spa, deck, wall, drainage)
  • What went wrong, in a few sentences, and when it started
  • The names of the parties: owner, builder or subcontractor, and any attorneys or insurers, so a conflict check can be run first
  • Your deadline, such as a mediation, expert disclosure or trial date
  • Photos or documents if you have them. Please send original photo files rather than copies forwarded by text message, because texting strips the date and location information that makes them useful as evidence.

Who Randy works with

Attorneys for plaintiffs and defendants, insurance carriers and adjusters, homeowners associations and property managers, and homeowners. Owners who are planning or building a project can also retain him as an owner's representative or for a plan and hydraulic peer review before construction.
