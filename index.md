## Common attorney inquiries

**What are your primary areas of expertise as a pool expert witness?**
Swimming pool structural defects, hydraulic design failures, soils / geotechnical hillside pool interfaces, pool plaster inspections and failures, safety and compliance reviews, and NEC Article 680 pool grounding and bonding.

**Do you handle both plaintiff and defense assignments?**
Yes. Randy provides unbiased forensic evaluations for defense counsel, insurers, HOAs, property owners and plaintiff attorneys, based on industry standards, building codes and physical evidence.

**What geographical areas do you serve?**
Based in Costa Mesa (Orange County), serving the Los Angeles, San Diego and Riverside areas, and available for site inspections, depositions and trial testimony nationwide.

**Why is NEC Article 680 compliance critical in swimming pool lawsuits?**
NEC Article 680 governs electrical installations in and around pools, hot tubs and spas. Improper equipotential bonding or incorrect grounding of pumps and lights creates shock and electrocution hazards. Randy evaluates bonding and grounding compliance in personal-injury and construction defect cases.
