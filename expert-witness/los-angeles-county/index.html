Common pool disputes in Los Angeles County

  • Hillside and vanishing-edge pools. Steep lots, caissons, grade beams and catch basins: where most high-dollar Los Angeles pool failures start. Read more.
  • Pools inside buildings. Podium, rooftop and pedestal pools over garages and living space, where a leak becomes a building problem.
  • Fire-damaged pools. Evaluating shells, plaster, plumbing and equipment after wildfire, and separating fire damage from pre-existing defects.
  • Pool decks and hardscape. Deck cracking, drainage toward structures and paver and stone failures around the pool.
