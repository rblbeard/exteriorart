Retainer

A retainer of $3,500 is required to begin an engagement. It is applied toward billed hours, not charged on top of them.

Billing

  • Time is recorded in tenths of an hour and shown line by line on each invoice.
  • Invoices are issued monthly and are due within 30 days.
  • Payment is made to Randy Beard. The remittance address is provided on engagement.

How an engagement starts

  1. You email or call with the property city, the issue and your deadline, and the names of the parties so a conflict check can be run.
  2. Randy confirms there is no conflict and discusses the scope of the work.
  3. The fee schedule is signed and the retainer is paid.
  4. Work begins: document and photo review, a site inspection where needed, then a written report if one is wanted.
